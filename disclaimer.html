Talk to Your Doctor Before Starting

You should consult your doctor or a qualified healthcare provider before starting any new supplement or herbal protocol, especially if you:

  • Are pregnant or nursing
  • Are taking prescription or over-the-counter medications
  • Have a diagnosed medical condition
  • Have had surgery recently or have a scheduled procedure
  • Are under 18 or are selecting for a child
  • Have known allergies to plants, herbs, or supplements

Supplement & Herb Safety

Natural does not mean risk-free. Herbs and supplements can interact with medications, affect lab results, and cause side effects in certain individuals. Dose ranges provided on this platform are general commonly-used ranges and may not be appropriate for your individual situation.

We always recommend introducing one new supplement at a time and giving it at least two weeks before evaluating its effect. This helps you identify any sensitivities and attribute changes to a specific item.
