Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Root of Remedy ("we," "our," or "us") operates rootofremedy.com and provides AI-powered natural wellness services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

⚠️ Root of Remedy provides general wellness information for educational purposes only. Nothing on this platform constitutes medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any new supplement or herbal protocol.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you create a subscription
  • Payment information: Processed securely by Stripe — we never store your card details
  • Usage data: How you interact with the platform (pages visited, features used)
  • Health information you voluntarily share: Symptoms, wellness goals, and journal entries you type into our AI consultant or journal features
  • Device information: Browser type, IP address, and general location

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve our AI wellness consultation service
  • Personalize your wellness plans and recommendations
  • Process your subscription payments via Stripe
  • Send transactional emails (welcome, billing, account updates)
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Improve our platform and content

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your health information for advertising.

3. AI Conversations

When you use our AI Remedy Consultant, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. Anthropic processes these messages according to their own privacy policy. We do not permanently store your conversation history on our servers beyond your current session.

Please do not share highly sensitive personal medical information (such as specific diagnoses, medications, or personally identifying health details) in the AI chat.

4. Pinterest Integration

We use the Pinterest API to publish wellness content to our Pinterest business account (@rootofremedy). We only access Pinterest data related to our own business account for content publishing purposes. We do not access, collect, or store any Pinterest user data from third parties.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use localStorage in your browser to store your authentication token so you can access your dashboard across sessions. We may use basic analytics to understand how our site is used. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption for all data transmission and secure token-based authentication for dashboard access. Your payment information is handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your subscription is active. If you cancel your subscription, we may retain your information for up to 90 days for accounting and legal purposes, after which it is deleted.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@rootofremedy.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Root of Remedy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Anthropic — AI responses (anthropic.com/privacy)
  • Vercel — website hosting (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Pinterest — content distribution (policy.pinterest.com)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
